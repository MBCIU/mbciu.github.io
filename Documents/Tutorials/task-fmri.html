Task fMRI analysis

Design, first-level modelling and interpretable contrasts

Research training guide · Updated 14 October 2026. Examples use placeholder paths and require adaptation to your acquisition and installed software. Test one participant before scaling to a cohort.

1. Audit events and timing

Match each BOLD run with its event table. Verify onsets and durations in seconds relative to the start of the imaging run. Check discarded volumes, scanner triggers, missed responses and run-specific repetition time. Plot events against the acquired time series before building the model.

2. Preprocess and inspect

Choose a consistent preprocessing workflow and review registration, distortion correction and motion. If inputs are already preprocessed, explicitly disable duplicated operations in downstream software. Keep task runs and sessions distinguishable throughout the workflow.

3. Build a first-level model

In FSL FEAT, define explanatory variables for the scientific conditions, their haemodynamic response treatment, nuisance regressors and contrasts. For the custom three-column timing format, each row gives onset, duration and amplitude.

# Example condition timing file: onset_seconds duration_seconds amplitude
12 2 1
36 2 1
60 2 1

Set the high-pass filter with respect to the design. Inspect the design matrix, regressor correlations and contrast estimability. Decide how motion outliers and incorrect trials are handled before cohort analysis.

4. Combine runs and participants

Review first-level model residuals and registration before combining runs. Use a model appropriate to repeated observations within participants and the group question. Specify covariates and inference procedures in advance. Preserve effect estimates and their uncertainty, rather than exporting thresholded activation images alone.

5. Practical QC exercise

For one pilot participant, compare the event timeline with the design matrix, inspect a contrast image over the anatomical scan, and explain what a positive contrast value means. Then check whether that explanation remains correct when conditions are reordered. Record task completion, usable runs, motion and exclusions in the cohort manifest.
