MRS fitting and voxel placement

Spectral quality, anatomical context and reproducible quantification

Research training guide · Updated 14 October 2026. Examples use placeholder paths and require adaptation to your acquisition and installed software. Test one participant before scaling to a cohort.

1. Assemble the inputs

Collect metabolite data, unsuppressed water reference when acquired, sequence metadata and the anatomical image used for placement. Record field strength, pulse sequence, echo/repetition times, voxel geometry and processing history. Use a basis set matched to the actual sequence and acquisition.

2. Review voxel placement

Overlay the voxel on anatomy in axial, sagittal and coronal views, with a 3D view if helpful. Check the intended region, rotation, tissue composition and proximity to skull, CSF and susceptibility boundaries. Verify coordinate conventions before interpreting an overlay. Apparent rotation in a single plane is not sufficient evidence of a misplaced voxel.

For a cohort, inspect individual placements before summarising overlap. Report the denominator as successfully reconstructed placements and list missing subject/session pairs separately.

3. Fit a prepared spectrum

The example assumes converted, preprocessed NIfTI-MRS data and a validated basis set. It does not replace coil combination, transient alignment or rejection where required.

fsl_mrs --data metab.nii.gz \
  --basis /path/sequence_matched_basis \
  --h2o water_reference.nii.gz \
  --output /path/new_fit_output --report

For water-scaled estimates, evaluate tissue fractions and relaxation assumptions for your acquisition. Preserve the quantification settings with the results.

4. Review the fit

Inspect the measured spectrum, model, baseline and residuals together. Review linewidth, SNR, frequency drift and metabolite uncertainty using a prespecified protocol. Investigate lipid contamination and unexpected baseline structure. Do not apply a universal uncertainty cutoff without considering the metabolite and how selection may bias group comparisons.

Export concentrations, uncertainty, scaling method, voxel location, tissue fractions where available and QC outcome. Keep missing spectra distinct from fitted spectra excluded after review.
